# 🏨 Build “Luxora” – Your Hotel WhatsApp AI Concierge on Lyzr Studio

This README provides a complete step-by-step guide to help you build your personalized WhatsApp AI assistant, **Luxora**, using **Lyzr Studio**.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step: Build “Luxora” on Lyzr Studio

### ✅ Step 1: Sign in to Lyzr Studio
- Visit [https://studio.lyzr.ai](https://studio.lyzr.ai)
- Log in with your credentials or create a free account.

---

### ✅ Step 2: Create a New Agent
- Click **"Create New Agent"** or **"New Project"**.
- Name your agent:  
  `🏨 Luxora – Hotel WhatsApp AI Concierge`
- Choose **“Conversational AI”** as the agent type.

---

### ✅ Step 3: Define the Prompt (Agent Personality)
In the **Prompt** section, paste this:

```text
You are Luxora, the 24/7 WhatsApp Concierge and Operations Assistant for [Hotel Name]. You greet guests with warmth, answer questions, manage bookings, handle room service and housekeeping requests, offer local recommendations, upsell amenities, and collect feedback — all with elegance and efficiency. You understand tone and context, and personalize responses based on guest type (e.g., business, family, honeymoon, VIP). You are multilingual, discreet, and never share private info. Always ensure guests feel cared for, supported, and delighted — as if they were at a 5-star property.
```

- ✅ Replace `[Hotel Name]` with your actual property name.

---

### ✅ Step 4: Add Tools (Optional Enhancements)
Integrate useful tools with Lyzr:

- Google Sheets – for managing guest bookings/logs
- Webhooks/APIs – for live data like room availability
- WhatsApp API – for connecting Luxora to WhatsApp using Twilio

---

### ✅ Step 5: Add Knowledge or FAQs
- Upload hotel brochures, spa menus, check-in policies, dining hours, etc.
- Add common questions manually:
  - “What time is breakfast?”
  - “How do I book a massage?”

---

### ✅ Step 6: Test the Agent
- Click **“Test Agent”**
- Try prompts like:
  - “Can I get extra towels sent to Room 307?”
  - “Do you offer airport shuttle service?”
  - “What’s the best local restaurant nearby?”

✔️ Check that Luxora responds:
- Politely and professionally
- With contextual understanding
- Tailored to guest type (VIP, family, solo)

---

### ✅ Step 7: Connect to WhatsApp via Twilio
- Sign up at [https://www.twilio.com/whatsapp](https://www.twilio.com/whatsapp)
- Set up a WhatsApp Business number
- Connect Lyzr to Twilio using:
  - Webhook integration **or**
  - Lyzr Chat API

🎯 Link Luxora’s responses to WhatsApp messages.

---

### ✅ Step 8: Deploy and Monitor
- Publish your agent live on WhatsApp
- Monitor guest interactions in Lyzr dashboard
- Use analytics to refine replies, tone, and coverage

---

### ✅ Step 9: Add Memory (Premium Feature)
- Enable guest profile memory:
  - Name, room number, preferences, stay history
  - e.g., “Mr. Ahmad prefers a sea-facing view and late breakfast”

---

### ✅ Step 10: Upskill Luxora Over Time
- Train Luxora to respond to seasonal events, hotel packages, and promo offers
- Add upselling examples:
  - “Would you like to upgrade to a suite with a jacuzzi?”

---

## 🎓 Summary

Luxora is your 24/7 intelligent front desk assistant that enhances guest experience, automates operations, and supports hotel staff using AI.
